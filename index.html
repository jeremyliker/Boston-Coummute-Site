SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Read the numbers in context

How these times were produced

MA Town Finder used RAPTOR, a transit-routing algorithm, with the MBTA's published GTFS schedule feed to calculate morning-peak journeys. This is schedule modeling, not a survey or GPS study. Getting to the origin station and the final walk are excluded.

The supplied CSV is the numerical source for this site. Typical time is the median scheduled journey for a town–destination pair; the headline median then gives each eligible town equal weight.

Dates and provenance

Sources accessed September 22, 2026. The supplied screenshot says September 17, 2026; the retrieved dataset page says September 11, 2026. This discrepancy is unresolved. Neither establishes the timetable service date. The precise arrival window, service day, and GTFS version for this file are not documented in the supplied materials.

What this data cannot tell us

  • The file covers 81 municipalities, not all Massachusetts communities. Transit-oriented coverage creates selection bias; absence does not establish that a town lacks transit.
  • Towns have equal weight. No employee distribution or population weighting is supplied, so the site cannot estimate how many employees benefit.
  • Actual delays, disruptions, crowding, fares, accessibility needs, and first/last-mile travel are not measured.
  • Results cover morning-peak schedules. Return trips, weekends, and other times are not compared.
  • There are no observed variability estimates or statistical confidence intervals. Fastest-to-typical differences are not reliability estimates.
  • Six destination-containing rows use zero as a marker. They are excluded from journey statistics, not treated as free or instantaneous travel.
  • Limited-service rows have fewer than three peak departures. They are excluded by default and clearly flagged when included.

Privacy by design

Only public municipality-level data is used. No names, contacts, medical details, employee addresses, precise personal locations, personal uploads, geolocation, or tracking forms are collected by this dashboard. Filters stay in this page.
